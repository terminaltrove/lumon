# Controls

Press `?` in the game to see this list. `c`, `w`, `m` and `l` only work in a
game you run yourself. They do nothing on the public server.

## Refining

- `↑` `↓` `←` `→` or `h` `j` `k` `l` - Move the cursor
- `f`, `shift`+arrows or `H` `J` `K` `L` - Take the cluster under the cursor, and keep it while you move
- `1`-`5` - Choose a bin
- `o` - Open the selected bin
- `enter` or `space` - Refine the cluster under the cursor
- Mouse - Click a digit to refine it, or a bin to select it
- Wheel - Scroll the grid

## The terminal

- `s` - Who has refined what
- `+` / `-` - Spread the digits further apart, or bring them back in
- `t` - Next colour theme
- `a` - Who made this
- `i` - Screensaver
- `n` - Hide or show the floor news
- `m` - Host a LAN game, or join one
- `b` - Broadcast a preset Lumon phrase above your cursor
- `?` - These controls
- `q` - Back out of the current view; on the board, choose another file or quit
- `ctrl+c` - Quit from anywhere
- `c` - A computer refiner joins you; with every desk full, they all go
- `w` - Get up and watch the computer refiners work, or sit back down
- `1`-`4`, `tab`, `0` - While watching: follow that desk's refiner, the next one, or whoever the camera picks

## Choosing a file

- `↑` `↓` or `j` `k` - The next file, or the next row in the grid
- `←` `→` - Jump a letter, or a tile in the grid
- `+` / `-` - The file with the most refiners on it, or the fewest
- `v` - The rolodex, the list, or the grid of files
- `enter` - Join the file you are on
- `n` - Open a new file
- `l` - Host a LAN game, or join one
- `c` - How many computer refiners join you

## Floor news and phrases

Floor news runs between the two lines above the board. It shows arrivals,
finished files and quarterly winners for 30 seconds each, with an occasional
Lumon notice. Every open file sees the same headline. Press `n` to hide it.

Phrases (`b`) come from a fixed list. You can't type your own.
